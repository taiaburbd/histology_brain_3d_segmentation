## Overview

## Key Functionalities

### Reassigning Region Labels

- **Label Reassignment**: This process involves changing the labels assigned to different segmented regions within the images. It's crucial for ensuring that the labels match expected standards or new classification schemes.

### Metadata Modification

- **Metadata Updates**: After reassigning labels, corresponding metadata within the image files' headers is updated to reflect these changes accurately. This ensures that downstream processes and analyses can correctly interpret the data.

### NIfTI to NRRD Conversion

- **Format Conversion**: Converts images from the NIfTI format, commonly used in medical imaging, especially in research, to the NRRD format, which is often preferred for certain types of visualization software and archival purposes.
