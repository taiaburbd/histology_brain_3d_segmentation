
# Visualization Techniques Documentation for `09.Visualization.ipynb`

This document provides an overview of the functionalities implemented in the `09.Visualization.ipynb` notebook, designed for advanced visualization of medical images. The notebook includes methods for displaying all regions, individual regions, and comparing images across different planes and dimensions.

## Overview

The `09.Visualization.ipynb` notebook offers a suite of visualization tools to explore and analyze medical imaging data in both 2D and 3D formats. It is tailored for detailed inspections of segmented regions, comparisons between images and their corresponding ground truths, and comprehensive views combining multiple regions and planes.

## Key Functionalities

### Multi-Planar 2D and 3D Views

- **All Region Visualization**: Displays all segmented regions within a single comprehensive view, enabling a global assessment of the segmentation.
- **Single Region Focus**: Isolates and presents individual segmented regions, allowing for detailed examination of specific areas.
- **3D Views**: Offers three-dimensional visualizations of segmented regions, providing a spatial perspective that enhances the understanding of the structures.
- **Multi-Planar Views**: Includes axial, sagittal, and coronal plane visualizations, which are essential for medical imaging analyses.

### Comparative Visualization in 2D

- **Image and Ground Truth Comparison**: Enables side-by-side comparisons between the original medical images and their corresponding ground truth annotations across the axial, sagittal, and coronal planes.
- **Six Window Layout**: Facilitates a simultaneous view of six different images or conditions, aiding in comparative analyses and multi-faceted evaluations.
- **24 Region Label Overview**: Displays 24 different segmented regions in a single view, allowing quick comparisons and assessments across multiple regions.
